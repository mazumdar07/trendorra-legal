How to delete your data

Last updated: 5 November 2026

You can remove your data from Trendorra at any time. There are three ways.

1. Disconnect Instagram in Trendorra

In Trendorra, open Settings and choose Disconnect on the Instagram account. The access key is deleted immediately and the synced Instagram data is deleted within 30 days.

2. Delete your Trendorra account

In Trendorra, open Settings and choose Delete account. Your Instagram access key is erased at once and your data is then deleted shortly after.

3. Remove Trendorra from Facebook

  1. Open Facebook, then Settings and privacy, then Settings.
  2. Choose Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  3. Select Trendorra 2.0 and choose Remove.
  4. Optionally tick the option to delete the data Trendorra received. Facebook then notifies Trendorra, and we delete the data linked to your Facebook login.
