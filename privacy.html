Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Trendorra is a platform where brands and creators collaborate, with clear metrics about audience quality and expected results. This policy explains what personal data Trendorra collects, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Trendorra, operated by Shubham Mazumdar (founder). Contact for privacy requests: smazumdar021099@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address and/or phone number, name, and whether you use Trendorra as a brand or a creator.
  • Creator profile: information you enter, such as niche, city, languages and rates.
  • Instagram data, only if you connect your account. When you choose to connect, you sign in with Facebook and approve specific permissions. We then read your Instagram professional account's profile (username, name, picture, follower and following counts, biography), your posts (captions, dates, type, links), insights for those posts (reach, views, saves, shares, likes, comments), account-level insights, and, for accounts with at least 100 followers, audience breakdowns (age, gender, location) as provided by Meta.
  • Login and technical data: your Facebook login access key (stored encrypted, never shown or logged), the Pages you approved, and basic logs such as time of connection and sync.
  • Waitlist data: email address, role, optional phone and city, and the consent you gave.

Why we collect it

  • To show brands a creator's verified audience quality, audience fit and expected results, and to show creators their own statistics.
  • To run accounts, offers and collaborations, and to keep the service secure.
  • To contact you about your account or the waitlist.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use Instagram data for advertising or to train AI models. We do not scrape Instagram; we only receive data you authorise through Meta's official interfaces.

Who can see Instagram data

The creator who connected the account can always see it. Brands on Trendorra may see the scores and statistics we compute from it for creators who have chosen to make their profile visible.

Who processes your data

Hosting, database, email and SMS providers act on our behalf. Meta provides Instagram data through its platform under its own terms. Provider names and storage location: to be confirmed before public launch.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect Instagram at any time in Trendorra settings; we then delete the access key immediately.
  • Ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by emailing smazumdar021099@gmail.com.
  • Remove Trendorra at any time in Facebook under Settings, Business Integrations.
  • See how to delete your data.

How long we keep it

After you disconnect, synced Instagram data is kept for up to 30 days in case you reconnect, then deleted. If you delete your account or request deletion, your access key is erased at once and your data is deleted shortly after. Retention for other data is to be confirmed.

Security

Access keys are encrypted at rest. Data is transferred over encrypted connections.

Complaints

Write to smazumdar021099@gmail.com. A formal grievance officer for Indian law will be named before public launch.
